## PharmaCom: Your Quick Guide to Medications and Healthcare

People waste time going to pharmacies that don't have the medication they need. PharmaCom is an app designed to solve
that problem.

**How it Works:**

PharmaCom takes a description of your symptoms, estimates their severity (green, yellow, red), and recommends
over-the-counter medications from its internal database.  It then displays nearby pharmacies on a map, showing you
which ones have the product in stock.

**Key Features:**

*   **Symptom Assessment:**  Describe your symptoms and receive a severity level (green, yellow, or red).
*   **Medication Recommendations:** Get suggestions for over-the-counter medications based on your symptoms.
*   **Pharmacy Locator:** Find nearby pharmacies with real-time stock information displayed on a map.
*   **Medical Consultation:** For non-minor symptoms, PharmaCom recommends a medical consultation and allows you to
schedule an appointment at a clinic.
*   **Emergency Assistance:** In urgent situations, the app directly contacts 112 (emergency services).
